Mahīśāsaka Vinaya 五分律（彌沙塞）

Bhikkhu Vibhaṅga 比丘尼分

Pācittiya 36

SC 5 Note by Bhikkhu Sujato: Pācittiya 36 is missing. The Mahīśāsaka Pāṭimokkha (T23 no. 1423 p. 210a22) has: 若比丘尼故奪畜生命。波逸提. The rule numbering of the Taishō edition from here on in is therefore out by one . I have corrected the numbering in the rule headings to accord with the Pātimokkha. The Taishō numbering is retained in the notes at the end of the vaggas.
