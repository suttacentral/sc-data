# Guide

*   [`difficulties.json`](difficulties.json)
    These represent how difficult a sutta is, from 1-3, with 3 being the hardest.
    On the site, they are represented by images of a seedling through a tree, such as in the top right corner of suttaplex cards in <https://suttacentral.net/dn-silakkhandhavagga>

*   [`epigraphs.json`](epigraphs.json)
    These are the sutta quotes on the home page, e.g.
    <img width="482" alt="screenshot of homepage epigraph section" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6897f113-d526-4b99-81db-22d504f9ca6f" />

*   [`map_data.json`](map_data.json)
    These geographic coordinates make up the interactive map in <https://suttacentral.net/map>
